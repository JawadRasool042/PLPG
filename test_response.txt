### Steps to Start with AI/ML:

1. **Understand the Basics:**
   - Learn fundamental concepts such as supervised vs. unsupervised learning, classification, regression, and clustering.

2. **Mathematics and Statistics:**
   - Brush up on linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics, as these are crucial for understanding algorithms.

3. **Programming Skills:**
   - Get comfortable with Python, as it is the most widely used language in AI/ML. Libraries like NumPy, pandas, and Matplotlib are essential.

4. **Online Courses:**
   - Consider enrolling in online courses such as:
     - Coursera: "Machine Learning" by Andrew Ng
     - edX: "Introduction to Artificial Intelligence"
     - Udacity: "AI Programming with Python Nanodegree"

5. **Hands-on Practice:**
   - Work on projects to apply what you learn. Start with simple tasks like predicting house prices or classifying images.

6. **Data Sets:**
   - Use platforms like Kaggle to find datasets for practice and participate in competitions.

7. **Stay Updated:**
   - Follow AI/ML blogs, podcasts, and research papers to keep up with the latest trends and advancements.
